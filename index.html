about

Hi, I'm Kasen. By day, I am a technical program manager at NVIDIA, with an MS in AI/ML from UCLA and a BS in Mathematics and Computer Science from UCSD.

I work on the SW GPU NPI team, where I help plan GPU compute capacity and allocation and build agents and tools that make the work faster. Before NVIDIA, I led an omnichannel agent program at ServiceNow that fused chat and voice into one experience, after shipping standalone voice agents and an LLM-powered analytics tool called AI Data Explorer. I like finding quiet places where AI and automation can make everyday work feel lighter.

My favorite ideas sit where finance, tech infrastructure, AI, and agents overlap, and that is where most of my side projects end up. When I close the laptop, I am usually watching markets, traveling somewhere new, cooking, swimming, or behind a camera, always up for a new place, idea, or conversation.
